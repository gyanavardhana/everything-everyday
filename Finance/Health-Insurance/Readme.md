## The five rules that matter most
1. **Declare everything in writing on the proposal form.** Telling the agent doesn't count.
2. **Compare the price after medical loading,** not the brochure price.
3. **Read the policy wording, not the brochure or a chatbot summary.** Quote page numbers back to the insurer.
4. **Look at complaints per 10,000 claims,** not only the claim settlement ratio.
5. **Get every promise in writing** (underwriting decision, add-on prices, hospital list) before paying.
