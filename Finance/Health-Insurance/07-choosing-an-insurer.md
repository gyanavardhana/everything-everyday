# Choosing an Insurer

## Which numbers to look at
| Metric | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **Complaints per 10,000 claims** | Best single signal of how claims are handled | Look at the 3-year trend, not one year |
| **Claim settlement ratio (by count)** | Basic sanity check | Partial payments still count as "settled" |
| **Incurred claim ratio** | Whether the insurer pays out a fair share of premiums | Extremes in either direction are a warning |
| **Volume of consumer-court cases** | Real-world failure patterns | Big insurers have more cases simply from size; compare against their size |
| **Regulator actions, data leaks, hospital disputes** | Operational risk | Search news for the insurer + "IRDAI" / "cashless suspended" |

Source: Ditto Data Lab (https://joinditto.in/health-insurance/data-lab/) compiles these from IRDAI annual reports.

## How to compare plans fairly
1. Download each plan's **prospectus, policy wording, CIS, rate chart and proposal form** from the insurer's site. Note the version and UIN.
2. Build one table: price, waits per condition, room rent, co-pay, consumables, restoration, bonus, pre/post days, network at **your** hospitals.
3. Compare **final quotes after loading**. List prices mislead when conditions exist.
4. Price at the **next age band** too; floaters are often priced on the eldest member.
5. Decide on a rule **in advance**, e.g. "pick the insurer with fewer complaints unless the other is ₹X/year cheaper". This keeps a sales pitch from deciding for you.

## Fixed plans vs build-your-own plans
- **Fixed variants:** easy to compare, fewer surprises.
- **Modular (many add-ons):** can be cheaper and tailored, but add-on prices are often unpublished and new products have no claims history.

## Agent vs buying direct
| Agent | Direct (website/branch) |
|---|---|
| Can help at claim time, **if** they're good and still around | Often 10–15% cheaper ("discount in lieu of commission") |
| Paid by commission; may push certain products or add-ons | You must fill the form and handle claims yourself |
| Their spoken promises aren't binding | Everything is on paper by default |

Whichever you choose: get promises **in writing**, fill the form yourself, and ask "what happens if my agent leaves?".

## Avoiding sales spam while researching
- Use one channel per insurer.
- Read official PDFs and rate charts instead of submitting "get quote" forms with your phone number.
