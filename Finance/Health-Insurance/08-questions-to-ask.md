# Questions to Send Any Insurer (Template)

> Ask for **written** answers, and refer to the policy wording page where possible.
> Replace the [brackets] with your own details. Attach recent medical reports.

## About us
- Policy wanted: [floater / individual] for [who], PIN [ ].
- Members and conditions: [age, condition, medication] for each person.
- Existing cover: [other policies].

## What to quote
- Plan [name], sum insured [₹X and ₹Y], no co-pay, no deductible, add-ons [list].
- If useful, quote it two ways: with and without wait reductions.

## A. Price
1. Total yearly premium, split into base, each add-on, and loading per condition.
2. Premium at the next age band.
3. Which discounts apply, and what's the maximum combined?
4. Extra cost of paying in instalments? Are remaining instalments deducted from a claim?
5. Which add-ons are charged only for the first few years?

## B. Waiting periods
6. From which month is each declared condition covered?
7. Is any condition on both the PED and specified-disease lists? Which wait applies?
8. For an old surgery without records: is a written declaration plus a current specialist note enough?
9. Are complications of a PED treated as part of the PED?
10. After the waits, will every declared condition be fully covered with no permanent exclusion?

## C. Medical check and approval
11. Which tests are needed for each person, and who pays?
12. Based on our attached reports, what loading should we expect?
13. Please give the underwriting decision in writing before we pay: accepted conditions, exclusions, loading per condition.
14. Does loading continue at renewal? Can it be reviewed later?
15. Can underwriting refuse any add-on?
16. Please send the completed proposal form before we pay.

## D. Hospital bills
17. Any room-rent limit? Any co-pay of any kind (age, city, hospital)? Any deductible?
18. Are consumables paid?
19. How do restoration and the no-claim bonus work after a claim?
20. Pre/post-hospitalisation days? Ambulance limit?

## E. Hospitals and claims
21. Is cashless active today for this plan at [list your hospitals]? Are any on the excluded list?
22. Who handles claims: you or a TPA? Reimbursement timeline?
23. If a bill exceeds another policy's limit, can we claim the balance from you? Which documents are needed?
24. Who is our contact for claims, and what if they leave?

## F. Future
25. Does time on an existing policy count towards your waits if we port?
26. Is renewal lifelong? Are we covered during the grace period?
27. If we increase the sum insured later, do waits restart on the extra amount?
28. Please send the final quote, the Customer Information Sheet and the policy wording for these exact options.
