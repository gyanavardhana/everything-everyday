# Government Schemes (Central or State)

Useful as an **extra safety net**, but:
- fixed package rates and a limited list of empanelled hospitals,
- more paperwork and approvals,
- some private hospitals are reluctant or slow to treat under them.

Don't count them as your main cover. Keep them for what they're good at (e.g. procedures on the package list), and have a personal policy as the base.
