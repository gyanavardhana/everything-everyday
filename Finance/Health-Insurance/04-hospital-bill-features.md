# Features That Change What You Actually Get Paid

## Room-rent limit: the biggest hidden cut
If your room costs more than the policy allows, **most of the bill** is cut in the same proportion, not just the room.
```text
Allowed room: ₹5,000/day   Room taken: ₹10,000/day   → 50% ratio
Doctor, OT, nursing, tests are paid at 50% too
(medicines, consumables and implants are usually exempt)
```
Prefer **"any room" / "no room-rent limit"**, especially when older people are covered.

## Co-pay
- Fixed % of every claim you pay. Look for hidden versions:
  - age-based (e.g. 20% after 60 or 61),
  - zone-based (treated in a costlier city than you're priced for),
  - for non-network hospitals,
  - for specific diseases.
- Ask: "Is there **any** co-pay, by age, city or hospital?"

## Consumables (non-medical items)
Gloves, PPE kits, masks, syringes and similar items. Without cover, you pay them yourself, often 5–15% of a bill. Some plans include them; others sell an add-on.

## Restoration vs no-claim bonus
| | Restoration | No-claim bonus |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Refills sum insured after it's used up in a year | Increases sum insured each claim-free year |
| Check | Same illness? Same person? How many times a year? | How much is lost after a claim (all, part, or only what was used)? |

## Other things to compare
- **Pre/post-hospitalisation days** (e.g. 30/60 vs 90/180): matters for chronic conditions with lots of tests and medicines.
- **Ambulance limit** (a few thousand rupees vs up to the sum insured).
- **Modern treatments** (robotic surgery, immunotherapy): included, sub-limited, or an add-on.
- **Domiciliary** exclusions (often diabetes, BP, arthritis).
- **Health check-up**: included from renewal, or an add-on.

## Paying by instalments
- Monthly/quarterly payment usually costs 3–5% more and removes long-term discounts.
- On a claim, many insurers **deduct all remaining instalments** from the payout.
- Multi-year policies paid upfront often get 10–15% off and lock the price, but a refund is harder if you want to switch.
