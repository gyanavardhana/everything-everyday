# Claims and Hospital Networks

## Cashless flow
```text
Admission → hospital insurance desk sends pre-authorisation → insurer/TPA approves
→ treatment → final bill → discharge approval → you pay only the non-covered part
```
IRDAI rules (2024): cashless decisions within **1 hour** of request, final discharge approval within **3 hours** of the hospital's request.

## Reimbursement flow
Pay the hospital → submit claim form, original bills, discharge summary, reports, prescriptions → within the deadline (often 15–30 days from discharge). Keep copies of everything.

## Checking the network properly
Insurer websites and aggregator sites often show **out-of-date lists**. Check in three ways:
1. Download the insurer's official network list and **note its date**.
2. Check the **excluded-hospital list** too.
3. **Call the hospital's insurance/TPA desk:** "Is cashless active right now for [insurer + plan]? How fast are approvals?" Note who you spoke to and the date.

Also ask the insurer to confirm your main hospitals **in writing**. Networks change because of hospital–insurer disputes; for example, hospital associations have suspended cashless for an insurer's customers for weeks at a time.

## More than one policy
- You can choose which policy to claim from first.
- If the bill is bigger than the first policy's limit, claim the **balance from the second** using attested copies and the first insurer's settlement letter.
- Always declare every existing policy on the proposal form.

## When a claim goes wrong
1. Insurer's grievance cell (in writing; keep the reference number).
2. Bima Bharosa portal: https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in
3. Insurance Ombudsman (free, no lawyer needed).
4. Consumer commission. Courts often reverse rejections that rest on vague "non-disclosure", but it takes years.
